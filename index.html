02 / The Materialization

Clothing Brand Collection
Spiritual Armour

10 garment views / always visible

Eclipse Essence transforms imagination into something that can be worn. Each garment carries original artwork, symbols, characters and cosmic imagery created from my own visual language. I see these pieces as spiritual armour—wearable expressions of protection, identity and inner strength, designed so that the person wearing them can connect with the protective energy and intention that inspired their creation.

“From imagination to protection — art does not have to remain on the canvas. It can become something you carry with you.”
03 / The World

Character Designs

04 foundations / animation + comics

These characters are foundations for the worlds I am building through animation and comic books. Every design begins as an idea and grows through experimentation, symbolism and time. There is no rush when it comes to building your own world or starting your own creative journey. Every day is a new day. Not everyone will see from your perspective or share your passion—and they do not have to. Keep creating, keep learning, and give your imagination the time it needs to become something only you could have made.

Keep Building

Your world does not have to arrive all at once.

One sketch can become a symbol. One symbol can become a garment. One character can become an entire universe. Trust the pace of your own imagination. The goal is not to create like everyone else—it is to keep giving your own perspective somewhere to exist.

04 / The signal

ART IS THE TOOL.
EXPERIENCE IS THE POINT.

Eclipse Essence grows from Bruce’s belief that creativity is bigger than technique or commerce. The work is a way to feel, question, experiment, connect, and give an inner idea somewhere real to exist.

Energy, frequency, vibration, programming, spirit, rebellion and synchronicity are presented here as Bruce’s personal creative language and philosophy—not as scientific claims or instructions about what anyone else must believe.

“Art is my passion, not my purpose.”
01 / Think

Question the programming.

Notice inherited patterns. Keep the parts that are true to you. Challenge the parts that are not. Individual thought is part of the creative process.

02 / Feel

Use the whole spectrum.

Highs, lows, darkness and light all carry information. The goal is not to erase experience; it is to transform experience into a visual language.

03 / Make

Authenticity before performance.

Selling art should not require selling a false version of yourself. Build from what is honest, specific and alive in your own point of view.

04 / Connect

Individuality + community.

Originality gives the work a voice. Community gives that voice somewhere to meet others. Create without demanding sameness.

06 / Creative principle

YOUR VISION DOESN'T NEED TO FIT A TEMPLATE.

Use references without becoming a copy. Build worlds without asking permission. Turn an abstract thought into a shape, a scene, a garment, an environment, a character, a structure or a complete visual system.

Illustrators

Push line, silhouette, character and visual storytelling into unexpected territory.

Designers

Translate concepts into identities, products, graphics and experiences with a clear visual voice.

Animators

Think in movement, transitions, rhythm, camera language and worlds that feel alive.

Architects

Speculate through mass, space, structure, atmosphere and environments that challenge the expected.
